# S09 — Individual evidence record

Blank editable template · v1.2.0 · local production candidate

This is your individual S09 evidence record. Complete the full P01 Routed Notes contract and the required P03 repair portfolio in one PDF. P02 is a conceptual capstone transfer; its full implementation is not another S09 completion gate.
Stop at 60 content minutes with an honest draft. Complete remaining required work before the actual deadline supplied by your teacher. No deadline or institutional mark is invented here.
A completed field, a model, an actual run, a saved PDF and a final Moodle submission are separate facts. This form checks record structure and awards no marks.
Use one filename: TW2026_S09_GROUP_Surname_Firstname.pdf. Do not submit a separate P03 PDF or upload node_modules. A ZIP is not required by default.
The HTML route is text-only. It cannot insert screenshot files. If your evidence includes captures, use the matching DOCX route, insert the genuine images with captions inside that document and export the same single PDF.

## Genuine captures in the same PDF

1. Open FORM_S09_EN_GB.docx in an authorised document editor. Find the field that cites your capture, such as link_history_trace or p03_qualification.
2. Use the editor’s picture insertion control, often Insert > Pictures > This Device or an equivalent label. Select your own sanitised capture. These labels are illustrative; they may differ in your editor.
3. Insert the image inside the document near its related response. Add a stable label and caption such as E01 — initial route and observed state. State what the capture establishes and what it cannot prove.
4. Add E01 to evidence_index. A typed filename does not attach an image. Every image or log cited as included must actually appear in the final PDF.
5. Review the source image for private browser tabs, credentials, tokens, account details and unnecessary personal data before inserting it. Do not upload private teacher reference code or a full Gemini conversation.
6. After exporting, open the exact saved PDF and inspect every page for clipped text, missing images and readable captions. Correct the document and inspect a successor if any page is defective.

## First export and later observations

1. Before first export: check identity, name, evidence locators, included captures, truthful pending work, privacy and your individual accuracy declaration. Do not claim that the first PDF has already been saved or reviewed.
2. Save the first PDF. The printed state may honestly be FIRST_EXPORT_CANDIDATE_NOT_YET_REVIEWED. A closed print dialog is not evidence that a file was saved.
3. Open the exact saved file and inspect every page. Keep its actual saved-file review in a separate personal or private record outside that already-saved snapshot.
4. Later, in the actual assigned S09 Moodle activity, check the title, attach the one PDF, wait for upload, save changes and complete the final submit/confirmation controls if they appear. Labels vary and were not observed here.
5. Only after the real submission, inspect and record the actual status, filename and timestamp. If the activity remains Draft, STOP: the submission is incomplete. Keep this post-submission observation outside the first-PDF prerequisite; do not invent a receipt inside the PDF.
