Before sending, exclude credentials, tokens, cookies, personal records, private paths, Moodle data and full conversations. Keep only the necessary excerpt. Review the exact outgoing text rather than assuming it is safe.

The independent check must directly test the chosen claim with a contract/source comparison, named test or actual observation. A second model paraphrase is not independent evidence. Label the class accurately: a source counterexample does not become a real browser trace.

UNKNOWN can be the justified verdict when the evidence cannot settle the AI claim. It does not confirm technical completion. No actual exchange remains PENDING. A separately authorised alternative requires a real prior teacher decision with a reference; writing a reference does not grant or authenticate approval.

Put the relevant actual exchange and independent check into the same final `TW2026_S09_GROUP_Surname_Firstname.pdf`. Keep the declaration truthful. The form checks record structure rather than code correctness, personal identity, teacher approval or Moodle acceptance.

The two public claim topics are URL/history and universal document fallback. Optional additional questions do not create an extra criterion, mark or substitute for the required work. Teacher-private counterexample answers remain private.
