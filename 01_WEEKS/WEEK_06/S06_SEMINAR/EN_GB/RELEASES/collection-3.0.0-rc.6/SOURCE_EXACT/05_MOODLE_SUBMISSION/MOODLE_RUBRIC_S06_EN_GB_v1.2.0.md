# S06 — assessment rubric

Maximum: **10 points**. The descriptors exactly match the public S06_RUBRIC_AND_HISTORICAL_MAPPING_EN_GB_v1.2.0.md. One individual PDF covers complete P02, the short separate file observation and one actual independently checked Gemini claim. Full P01/P03 are optional and are not required for the maximum standard mark. Agreement with Gemini earns no special credit.

## Experiment and technical result — 3 points

|Points|Descriptor|
|---:|---|
|0|missing, unsupported or materially contradictory result|
|1.5|substantive partial implementation or experiment with accurately stated missing results and boundaries|
|3|Original predictions and identified fixture; complete closed P02 translation; combined filters, literal-percent search, three sorts, ID tie-breaker, bounded projection, rejected inputs and valid recovery; short file reopen/reset result|

## Reproducible evidence — 2 points

|Points|Descriptor|
|---:|---|
|0|no traceable actual evidence; copied expected output does not become execution|
|1|some genuine evidence is traceable but a required witness or edit-boundary check is missing|
|2|Exact inputs, commands, actual ORM/SQLite and local HTTP excerpts, distinct counter/spy witness for zero calls, genuine named checks, protected-file result and traceable lifecycle stages|

## Mechanism — 2 points

|Points|Descriptor|
|---:|---|
|0|description is absent or contradicts the implementation/evidence|
|1|mechanism is partly explained but one important causal link is missing or unsupported|
|2|Explains validation before querying, exact tokens, fresh options/input preservation, options versus rows, literal substring/ASCII-case limit, deterministic ties and memory versus owned file/reset|

## Critical Gemini audit — 1.5 points

|Points|Descriptor|
|---:|---|
|0|no actual interaction/independent check, or synthetic practice presented as actual|
|0.75|genuine claim is identified but its independent check, verdict or limitation is incomplete|
|1.5|Actual bounded interaction, sanitised prompt, one selected claim, Observed/Inferred/Unknown classification, independent non-AI check, justified verdict, correction and limit|

## Limitations and reflection — 1 points

|Points|Descriptor|
|---:|---|
|0|missing reflection or unsupported universal claim|
|0.5|reflection is relevant but a limit, counterexample or next check is vague|
|1|Specific untested boundary, honest counterexample, mechanism-based reflection and personal exit ticket; no universal claim from a small test set|

## Completeness and format — 0.5 points

|Points|Descriptor|
|---:|---|
|0|missing/unreadable file or essential identity/declaration absent|
|0.25|readable PDF with a limited format/completeness defect|
|0.5|One readable inspected PDF, correct current identity/filename, all required fields, honest provenance and renewed declaration|

A separately granted teacher decision is assessed against its real documented scope. The form cannot grant or authenticate an alternative. Prerequisite blocks remain accurately identified and do not silently replace standard final evidence. FINAL_FIELDS_COMPLETE_NOT_VERIFIED does not award marks.

The CSV is a structured local descriptor record. No native Moodle CSV import capability is claimed. Enter these levels through the live platform only at the separately authorised administration stage. Verify decimal points, total10 and the student-visible wording.
