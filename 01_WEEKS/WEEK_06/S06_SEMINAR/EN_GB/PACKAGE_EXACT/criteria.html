S06 assessment criteria

One PDF covers P02, the small course file observation and bounded Gemini review. Complete P01/P03 implementations are not required for the maximum mark. Agreement with Gemini is not a grading target.

Criterion Maximum Full-credit evidence
Contract and prior prediction 1 Exact seed/query and a prediction recorded before execution
Closed query translation 2 Supported owner/archived/search combinations with closed validation and no arbitrary passthrough
Deterministic ordering and projection 1 All named sorts, the equal-key ID rule and bounded selected fields
Actual execution, rejection and recovery 2 Traceable ORM/SQLite and HTTP results; invalid requests distinguished from environment faults
File-lifecycle observation 1 Precisely labelled file reopen/reset result and its limitation
Checks and edit boundary 1 Genuine named test results and only the permitted implementation edit
Independent Gemini review 1 One actual bounded claim, independent check, justified verdict and limit
Reflection and traceability 1 Readable PDF, evidence identifiers and specific remaining limits

For a one-point criterion the levels are 0, 0.5 and 1; for two points they are 0, 1 and 2. Missing or unsupported evidence receives zero. A documented separate accommodation is assessed against the teacher’s actual decision, not an automatically granted exemption.

Local teaching candidate. Completeness is not correctness, native qualification or a Moodle receipt.
