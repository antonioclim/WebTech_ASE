# S06 assessment rubric and historical mapping

Maximum: 10 points. This locally audited v1.2.0 edition supplies the six groups agreed in the didactic contract; it does not evidence live institutional approval or a configured Moodle rubric. One individual PDF covers complete P02, the short separate file-lifecycle observation and one actual independently checked Gemini claim. Full P01/P03 are optional and are not required for the maximum mark. Agreement with Gemini earns no special credit.

| Group | Maximum | Full evidence | Partial evidence | No supported evidence |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Experiment and technical result | 3 | Original predictions and identified fixture; complete closed P02 translation; combined filters, literal-percent search, three sorts, ID tie-breaker, bounded projection, rejected inputs and valid recovery; short file reopen/reset result | 1.5: substantive partial implementation or experiment with accurately stated missing results and boundaries | 0: missing, unsupported or materially contradictory result |
| Reproducible evidence | 2 | Exact inputs, commands, actual ORM/SQLite and local HTTP excerpts, distinct counter/spy witness for zero calls, genuine named checks, protected-file result and traceable lifecycle stages | 1: some genuine evidence is traceable but a required witness or edit-boundary check is missing | 0: no traceable actual evidence; copied expected output does not become execution |
| Mechanism | 2 | Explains validation before querying, exact tokens, fresh options/input preservation, options versus rows, literal substring/ASCII-case limit, deterministic ties and memory versus owned file/reset | 1: mechanism is partly explained but one important causal link is missing or unsupported | 0: description is absent or contradicts the implementation/evidence |
| Critical Gemini audit | 1.5 | Actual bounded interaction, sanitised prompt, one selected claim, Observed/Inferred/Unknown classification, independent non-AI check, justified verdict, correction and limit | 0.75: genuine claim is identified but its independent check, verdict or limitation is incomplete | 0: no actual interaction/independent check, or synthetic practice presented as actual |
| Limitations and reflection | 1 | Specific untested boundary, honest counterexample, mechanism-based reflection and personal exit ticket; no universal claim from a small test set | 0.5: reflection is relevant but a limit, counterexample or next check is vague | 0: missing reflection or unsupported universal claim |
| Completeness and format | 0.5 | One readable inspected PDF, correct current identity/filename, all required fields, honest provenance and renewed declaration | 0.25: readable PDF with a limited format/completeness defect | 0: missing/unreadable file or essential identity/declaration absent |

A separately granted teacher decision is assessed against its real documented scope. The form cannot grant or authenticate an alternative. Prerequisite blocks remain accurately identified; they are not relabelled as expected student assertions. Labels such as FINAL_FIELDS_COMPLETE_NOT_VERIFIED do not award marks.

## Mapping from the eight v1.1.0 criteria

This mapping preserves the subject matter while changing the grouping. It is not an automatic point conversion and does not duplicate credit for the same observation.

| Historical v1.1.0 criterion | Historical maximum | v1.2.0 groups retaining the requirement |
|---|---:|---|
| Contract and prior prediction | 1 | Experiment and technical result; Reproducible evidence; Mechanism |
| Closed query translation | 2 | Experiment and technical result; Mechanism |
| Deterministic ordering and projection | 1 | Experiment and technical result; Mechanism |
| Actual execution, rejection and recovery | 2 | Experiment and technical result; Reproducible evidence |
| File-lifecycle observation | 1 | Experiment and technical result; Reproducible evidence; Mechanism; Limitations and reflection |
| Checks and edit boundary | 1 | Reproducible evidence; Completeness and format |
| Independent Gemini review | 1 | Critical Gemini audit |
| Reflection and traceability | 1 | Reproducible evidence; Limitations and reflection; Completeness and format |

The old maxima sum to 10 and the new maxima also sum to 10. No hidden full P01/P03 obligation or second C06 submission is introduced.
