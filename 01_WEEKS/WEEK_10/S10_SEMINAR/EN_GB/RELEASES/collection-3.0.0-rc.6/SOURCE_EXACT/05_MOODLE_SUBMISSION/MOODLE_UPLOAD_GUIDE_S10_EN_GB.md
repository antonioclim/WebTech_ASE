# Moodle upload guide — S10

Labels may vary by Moodle version, site language and institutional configuration.

1. Open the correct Web Technologies course and Week 10.
2. Open the S10 Assignment; stop if the title differs.
3. Select Add submission.
4. Upload one reviewed PDF only.
5. Wait for the filename and upload indicator.
6. Save changes.
7. Where enabled, select Submit assignment and confirm the statement.
8. Reopen the status page and verify final status, filename and timestamp.
9. Stop if the status remains Draft or the wrong file is shown.

Never upload the project tree, JSON draft, node_modules, credentials or a full AI conversation.
