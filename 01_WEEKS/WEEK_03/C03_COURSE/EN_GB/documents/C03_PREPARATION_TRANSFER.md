## After C03: bounded consolidation and optional depth

First revisit a prediction that changed. State the original expectation, the actual result, the language rule and a new input that could falsify your revised explanation. This creates a transferable reasoning step rather than a memorised answer.

Run or inspect canonical examples 02 and 03. For lookup, record the own-property result and the returned value. For copying, compare array, record and nested-object identities separately. Do not alter the preserved sources. Put any new experiments in a separate working file outside canonical/.
