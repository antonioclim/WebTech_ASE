# Moodle upload guide — S03

The exact labels may vary with the institutional Moodle version and language. This is a generic planning route, not verified online.ase.ro screen evidence.

1. Sign in through the authorised institutional route.
2. Open the correct course and Week 03.
3. Open the Assignment whose title identifies S03 Dataset Transformer CLI.
4. Read the deadline, time zone and submission status shown in Moodle. Do not infer them from this package.
5. Select **Add submission** or the closest equivalent.
6. Add exactly one PDF by drag-and-drop or the file picker.
7. Wait until the filename appears and the upload indicator has finished.
8. Select **Save changes**.
9. When a final-submit control is enabled, select **Submit assignment**, read the declaration and confirm once.
10. Re-open the Assignment and verify the final status, exact filename and timestamp.

**STOP:** if the status remains Draft, the wrong file is shown, the timestamp is absent or the page is ambiguous, do not claim final submission. Preserve evidence and contact the lecturer through the authorised channel.
