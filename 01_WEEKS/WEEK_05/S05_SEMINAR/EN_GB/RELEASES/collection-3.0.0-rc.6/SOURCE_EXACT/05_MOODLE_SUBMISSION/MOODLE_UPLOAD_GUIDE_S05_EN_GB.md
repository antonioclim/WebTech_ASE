# Upload one S05 PDF to Moodle

1. Sign in to `online.ase.ro`.
2. Open the correct course and Week 05.
3. Open the Assignment whose title contains `S05 — In-memory Task API`.
4. Verify the title before clicking **Add submission**.
5. Drag the one PDF into the file area or use the file picker.
6. Wait until the filename is visible.
7. Click **Save changes**.
8. If shown, click **Submit assignment** and confirm.
9. Verify status, filename and timestamp.

**STOP:** if the status remains Draft, do not assume final submission. Re-open the activity and use the final submit control if available, or report the exact labels shown.
