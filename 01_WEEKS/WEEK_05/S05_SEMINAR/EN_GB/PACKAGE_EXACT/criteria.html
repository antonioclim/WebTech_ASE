S05 assessment criteria

One individual PDF. Full P02/P03 implementations are optional and cannot be required for the maximum standard mark. A rejected Gemini claim can earn full credit when independently supported.

Criterion Maximum Full-credit evidence
Prediction and contract 1 Original prediction, identified fixture and coherent method/path/status/header/body matrix
P01 resource lifecycle 2 Correct bounded CRUD result, returned Location, preserved PATCH fields and empty 204 content
Validation, error ownership and recovery 2 Discriminating failure witnesses with preserved state and safe recovery
Tests and one-file edit boundary 1 Named baseline/objective/regression checks and truthful diff
Guided middleware observation 1 Correct identity, capture boundary and exactly-once limit; no P02 implementation required
Independent Gemini verification 1 Relevant actual claim tested with justified verdict and correction
Reflection and limitations 1 Specific remaining uncertainty tied to evidence class
Traceability and readable final PDF 1 Complete labelled evidence and correct private submission format

Total: 10 points. A complete form or green test summary is not proof of truthful, reproducible evidence. Partial and zero-credit descriptors are applied by the teacher.
