# S07 marking criteria

The proposed maximum is ten points. Optional full P01/P03 implementation is not required. A sound rejection of a generated claim can receive full credit. Appearance or a green label alone does not establish evidence.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Prediction and invariant | 1 |
| Successful managed booking | 2 |
| Failure, error identity and recovery | 2 |
| Transaction propagation and test interpretation | 1 |
| Required ADR | 1 |
| Gemini review | 1 |
| Limits and traceability | 1 |
| Complete readable PDF | 1 |

The eight fields of the ADR record context, resources, alternatives, decision, transaction boundary, repeated-action table, evidence and consequences. Missing the ADR leaves the required analysis incomplete even when the implementation passes its tests. Genuine booking/HTTP evidence and source-based design reasoning remain distinct.
