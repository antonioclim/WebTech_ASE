# Save one complete PDF and submit it privately

At minute 60 export the JSON draft. It preserves editable fields, not a Moodle submission. Finish outstanding P02 work and the required ADR before the separately stated deadline. The form has 55 fields in seven sections and all eight ADR fields are required for the standard route.

## HTML route

Open `evidence.html` from the extracted root. Enter predictions before observations. Export JSON frequently as the portable backup; optional browser storage may differ for local-file origins. Importing a draft marks it unverified and resets the declaration. Long fields are limited to 12,000 characters and short fields to 128; the import limit is 2,000,000 bytes. Screenshot files are not embedded in HTML: use labelled text excerpts or the DOCX route for images.

Use Check core draft during the meeting. Use Check final fields after P02 evidence, Gemini review and the ADR are complete. The checks validate structure only. UNKNOWN may be a defensible verdict on a bounded claim, not a declaration that an unexecuted required experiment ran. Any approved alternative must already have a separate teacher reference and exact scope. The form does not grant it.

Select Print final PDF. In the browser's print dialogue choose its PDF destination. Check the name suggested by the form; save as `TW2026_S07_GROUP_Surname_GivenName.pdf` with actual values. The page cannot force that name in every operating system. Open the resulting PDF and verify all seven sections, the full ADR and readable evidence. Long text must remain visible. Printing a draft is visibly NOT FINAL.

## DOCX route

Open `documents/S07_EVIDENCE_FORM.docx` in a suitable editor, complete the same labelled fields and retain their order. Insert only relevant labelled images or excerpts. Review the declaration after every substantive edit. Export to PDF and inspect every page. Pagination can change as you type; the blank form's page count is not a limit on your answer.

## Moodle route

Sign in to `online.ase.ro`, enter your Web Technologies course and open **TW2026 — S07: Transactional Booking and Architecture Decision Record** when the teacher has made it available. Use the site's file-submission action, choose the single PDF and save. Complete the final submission/declaration step when configured. Read the resulting submission status and reopen the uploaded PDF. A saved draft is not final submission. Actual labels and limits depend on the later site configuration; no live site was inspected during production.

Do not upload the source ZIP, JSON draft, editable DOCX, teacher package or a full Gemini conversation. Do not include credentials or private third-party data. Keep student identification and evidence out of the public repository. Preserve the final acknowledgement or status for your own records.
