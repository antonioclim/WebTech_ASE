# S07 submission

Use the single S07 Assignment provided by your teacher. Its deadline, opening date and upload interface are configured by the course owner; this candidate kit does not invent them.

1. Preserve your editable form and JSON backup before printing.
2. Complete the required P02 evidence, the P03-informed ADR and the actual Gemini audit. State unfinished checks honestly. Source reasoning is permitted for the ADR. Standard final P02 evidence requires genuine ORM SQLite and local HTTP observations. Synthetic practice is not automatic replacement evidence.
3. Review every section of the one evidence PDF. Expand long answers in the form before printing. Confirm that every page is readable and that no answer has been clipped. Browser PDF print acceptance is pending until tested in your actual browser.
4. Remove passwords, access tokens, private paths identifying other people and unnecessary personal information from prompts, screenshots and logs. Keep sufficient fixture, command, runtime and evidence class information for reproduction.
5. Name the PDF `TW2026_S07_GROUP_Surname_Firstname.pdf`, replacing GROUP, Surname and Firstname with your details.
6. Open the S07 Assignment and upload that one PDF. Check the displayed filename and submission state in the interface. Record the actual state; the kit cannot verify a Moodle upload.
7. Retain the local PDF and editable draft. Ask your teacher about a blocked submission using the configured course channel.

A selected teacher-alternative option records a declared pathway. It does not grant permission. Include the independent decision reference and the precise permitted scope if an authorised alternative applies.

The editable DOCX and blank PDF are templates. They are not completed student evidence or proof of upload.
