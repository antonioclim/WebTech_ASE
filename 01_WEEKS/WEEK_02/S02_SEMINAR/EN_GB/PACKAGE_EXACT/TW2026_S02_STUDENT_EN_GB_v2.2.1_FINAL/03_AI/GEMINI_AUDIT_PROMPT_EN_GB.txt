ROLE
Act as a reviewer of a CSS solution rather than the final authority.

CONTEXT
- fixed semantic HTML;
- the Responsive Card/Grid Reconstruction project;
- only public/styles.css may change;
- contract: 1 column below 40rem, 2 from 40rem and 4 from 64rem;
- inspection at 320, 768 and 1280 CSS pixels;
- visible focus plus prefers-reduced-motion;
- no frameworks, JavaScript layout, fixed widths or hidden overflow.

TASK
Review the CSS proposal line by line. For every rule return a table containing:
1. the target constraint;
2. the CSS mechanism;
3. the required test or browser evidence;
4. the risk or counterexample;
5. an ACCEPT / REJECT / REFORMULATE verdict.

CLASSIFICATION
Label every claim as OBSERVED, INFERRED or UNKNOWN.

RESTRICTIONS
Do not invent browser results, do not claim that tests passed without their output and do not change the HTML.

OPTIONAL MUSEUM EXTENSION: for every exhibit classify the SYMPTOM, MECHANISM, EVIDENCE, MINIMUM REPAIR and LIMIT separately. Do not label a technique ‘bad’ outside its context and do not assume that the absence of a scrollbar proves the absence of information loss.
