# Offline fallback evidence notice

The HTML laboratory and reference images are bounded teaching aids. They do not prove that the student's project ran, that a browser interaction occurred or that Windows, macOS, Firefox, Edge or Moodle acceptance passed.

Use them to continue the explanation when the exact runtime or browser is unavailable. Record the unavailable live gate as `UNKNOWN` and state the limitation explicitly.
