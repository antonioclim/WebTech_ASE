# Moodle upload guide - S02

This route is generic because the package does not contain a verified, current screenshot from `online.ase.ro`. Labels may differ between Moodle versions and institutional configurations.

1. Open the normal institutional login page and sign in yourself. Never place a password in the evidence form or a screenshot.
2. Open the correct Web Technologies course and Week 02.
3. Open the Assignment whose title identifies S02 Responsive Card/Grid Reconstruction.
4. Check that the expected submission is one PDF named `TW2026_S02_GROUP_Surname_Firstname.pdf`.
5. Choose **Add submission**, **Edit submission** or the equivalent file-upload action.
6. Drag the PDF into the file area or use the file-picker button.
7. Wait until the complete filename is visible. Do not navigate away during upload.
8. Choose **Save changes**.
9. If **Submit assignment** is present, select it and confirm the submission statement.
10. Re-open the Assignment and verify status, filename and timestamp.

**STOP if the status remains Draft.** Saving changes may not be the final submission action.

Do not upload the project folder, `node_modules`, a complete Gemini conversation, credentials or unrelated screenshots.
