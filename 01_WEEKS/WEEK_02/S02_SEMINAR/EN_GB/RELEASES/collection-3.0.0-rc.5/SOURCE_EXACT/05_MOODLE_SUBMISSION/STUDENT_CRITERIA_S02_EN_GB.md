# What a successful S02 submission demonstrates

Use these qualitative criteria to review your individual PDF. The Moodle assignment controls any marks or weights.

| Criterion | Evidence of satisfactory work | What weakens the submission |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Experiment and technical result | Named 1/2/4 states and the long-token edge case, with final source checks and bounded browser evidence | Missing states, a broken layout or concealed content |
| Reproducible evidence | Inputs, widths, outputs and observed or supplied status are explicit | Screenshots or unqualified claims without a reproducible method |
| Mechanism | Grid, Flexbox, intrinsic sizing, focus and motion are connected to the relevant rules and resolved observations | Generic explanations or source/observation contradictions |
| Critical AI audit | One claim is classified, checked independently and given a supported verdict; unavailable AI access is recorded honestly | Treating an AI response as the only evidence |
| Limits and reflection | Platform, simulation and automation limits are explicit, with a precise Business Informatics connection | Claiming checks you did not execute |
| Completeness and format | One legible PDF uses the required filename, honest declaration and final PDF review | Truncated evidence, missing identity, credentials or the wrong submission format |
