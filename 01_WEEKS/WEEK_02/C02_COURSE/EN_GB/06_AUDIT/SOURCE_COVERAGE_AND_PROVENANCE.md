in Week 02. U02 describes the JavaScript reading unit linked by the Week 03 C03 index.

modified to supply a linked invalid email state, corrected state and bounded source

for modified entries. It must not be described as an exact copy of the old source.

source verification. Original figure provenance remains the creation history.

The optional interface autopsy occupies screens 25 to 29 after the 60 minute core.

content and labels font size and motion simulations separately. It provides no
complete accessibility verdict. No legacy runtime, CDN or remote font was added.

pending gates. Local browser and rendered document checks are narrower evidence.
