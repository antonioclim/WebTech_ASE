Final completeness does not establish truth, successful code, teacher approval, PDF existence or Moodle receipt. All observations must be your own actual evidence with locators and an honest execution class.

Replace bracketed prompts with your text. Add space as needed; do not shrink evidence until unreadable. Insert actual screenshots near the related field and caption them; no screenshot is supplied or assumed. The HTML/JSON route is text-only. Imported text is an unverified draft, not a newly performed experiment. Recheck the declaration and exported PDF after every material edit.

Standard final result reports use ACTUAL_RECORDED: PASS only for actual successful checks, followed by command/name, source version and evidence locator. P03 checks separately name BASELINE, OBJECTIVE, REGRESSION and BUILD. Record all other outcomes honestly and retain draft when required completion is not achieved. Alternatives require prior explicit teacher authorisation and recorded replacement evidence; no exception is granted by this template.
