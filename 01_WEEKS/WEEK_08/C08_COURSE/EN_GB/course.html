State · 13–16 minutes

A handler reads a snapshot

Read the mechanism · Captured binding versus requested update

The handler belongs to one render and sees that render’s value. Calling the setter requests another update; it does not overwrite that captured binding midway through the handler. Read the mechanism · Captured stale value versus late settlement

A callback can retain a value from an earlier render because it closes over that render’s binding. Late settlement asks whether work that began earlier still owns publication. Cleanup · 45–47 minutes

Reverse and invalidate what you own

Cleanup precedes replacement setup and also runs on unmount. Clear the owned timer, remove the owned listener or abort the owned request. Where late settlement is possible, revoke its permission to publish too.
