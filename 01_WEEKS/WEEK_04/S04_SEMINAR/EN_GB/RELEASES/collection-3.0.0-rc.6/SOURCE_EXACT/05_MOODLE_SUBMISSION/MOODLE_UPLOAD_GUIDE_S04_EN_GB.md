# Moodle upload guide

1. Sign in to the institutional Moodle site in your normal browser.
2. Open the correct course and Week 04.
3. Open the Assignment whose title names S04 Multi-source Data Dashboard.
4. Verify the title before choosing **Add submission** or the local equivalent.
5. Drag exactly one PDF into the file area or use the file picker.
6. Wait until the filename is visible and the upload indicator has finished.
7. Choose **Save changes**.
8. If shown, choose **Submit assignment** and confirm.
9. Verify the final status, filename and timestamp.

STOP if the status remains Draft. Labels may vary on the live institutional instance; no live Moodle interface was accessed while producing this pack.
