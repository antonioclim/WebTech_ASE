# S04 — one final PDF on Moodle

Assignment title: **TW2026 — S04: Multi-source Data Dashboard and Interactive Task List**. Its actual opening and due dates are set by your teacher. The final deadline must allow the required P02 portfolio window. This guide does not assert that the Assignment has already been created.

## Prepare the final file

Complete both P01 and P02 in the same evidence form. Check the final fields and review every imported observation. Do not claim a browser or Gemini activity that did not occur. An alternative requires a separate explicit teacher authorisation with a recorded reference; the form cannot grant it.

From the HTML form choose Print final PDF, then your browser's PDF destination. The generated name is a suggestion, not control over the operating system. With DOCX, use your editor's PDF export and the same name:

```text
TW2026_S04_GROUP_Surname_GivenName.pdf
```

Replace GROUP and the names with your own. Reopen the saved PDF: check every section, long evidence excerpts, labels, page breaks, declaration and filename. A draft JSON, DOCX or ZIP is not the final submission. The HTML form does not embed screenshot image files; either use labelled text excerpts or insert captioned images in the DOCX route.

## Submit in the course

Sign in to online.ase.ro yourself. Open the Web Technologies course and the S04 Assignment bearing the title above. Read its actual dates, allowed file type and size. If it is missing, closed or different, contact the teacher; do not choose a different Assignment just to obtain an upload receipt.

Use Add submission, attach the one PDF and save changes. When the site displays a separate Submit assignment action, complete it and the declaration. Labels may vary with the configured Moodle version. A saved draft is not the final submission. Check the resulting submission status, file name and time, then reopen or download your submitted PDF from that page. Keep its confirmation privately.

The proposed size is up to 10 MB only where site/course limits permit it. Do not split the assessment into several files without teacher instruction. Never upload tokens, credentials, full conversations, other students' data or the private teacher kit.

## Correction rather than guesswork

A wrong file type or incomplete upload needs a technical correction, not a new scientific claim. Retain your private draft, explain the specific problem and follow the teacher's authorised reopening window. An incomplete implementation can be reported honestly; missing required evidence is a different issue. A complete form does not prove correctness, attendance or grading success.
