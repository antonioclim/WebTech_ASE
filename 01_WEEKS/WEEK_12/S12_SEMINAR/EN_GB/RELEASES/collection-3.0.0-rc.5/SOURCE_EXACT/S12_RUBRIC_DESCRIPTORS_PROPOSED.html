Status: PROPOSED DESCRIPTORS — OWNER ADOPTION PENDING. The existing local maxima below are preserved as proposals, not adopted grades. These nonnumerical levels describe observable evidence quality; they do not assign partial marks, pass thresholds, deadlines or penalties. Technical completion gates remain separate.

The ordinary seminar requirements and required actual-evidence routes remain unchanged. Supported partial work and reasoned UNKNOWN are described honestly; only an actual owner decision can adopt grading treatment or authorise a replacement.
