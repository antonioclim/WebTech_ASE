00–02 · Screen 1/24

Realtime starts with ownership, not transport

A fictional report export starts when the API accepts a command. Acceptance hands execution to a worker; the application interprets worker proposals as public projection; a registry chooses a recipient connection; the client dispatcher chooses a waiting caller. Cleanup belongs beside every owner that creates a resource, rather than becoming a substitute for local settlement. Before selecting a transport, draw this export across all five columns. Ask what event authorises each handoff and what witness remains missing. C04 completion and C05 service boundaries supply the prerequisites; C11 explains why a supplied client label is not trusted identity.
