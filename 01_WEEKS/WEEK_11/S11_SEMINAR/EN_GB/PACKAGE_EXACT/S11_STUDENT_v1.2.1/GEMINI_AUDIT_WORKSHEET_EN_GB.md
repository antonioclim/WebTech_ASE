**Blank candidate worksheet.** Record one actual bounded exchange using `GEMINI_PROMPT_EN_GB.txt`. No conversation, response or observation is prefilled. `FLAWED_PATCH_OR_CLAIM.txt` is an intentionally constructed teaching claim, not student evidence.

| Field in the 48-field form | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| `ai_route` | ACTUAL_RECORDED only for a genuine exchange. If access is blocked, retain PENDING and record the actual block in a draft. |
| `ai_claim` | One sanitised falsifiable claim; identify the source/trace and evidence class. |
| `ai_prompt` | Actual exchange date/time, visible model label or UNKNOWN, relevant prompt excerpt and sanitised locator. |
| `ai_response` | Relevant actual answer excerpt and locator. Never invent dialogue; use BLOCKED/NOT_EXECUTED honestly in a draft. |

| `ai_verdict` | ACCEPTED, REJECTED, PARTIALLY_ACCEPTED or UNKNOWN, justified by your independent evidence. |
| `ai_correction_limit` | Your narrow correction and remaining limit; any real access incident or genuine prior teacher-policy reference. |

WHERE YOU ARE: the supplied local lab and your own Gemini account/service, only when actually available and permitted by the course.
WHAT TO FIND: the prompt file, one minimal sanitised excerpt and one falsifiable claim.
EXACT ACTION: ask for a bounded critique, preserve only the relevant actual excerpt and then check it independently.
WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE: an actual response or an actual access block. Neither is guaranteed by this package.
WHAT TO RECORD: the table above and the independent check, not a complete conversation or a copied implementation.
DO NOT CONTINUE UNLESS: the excerpt has no passwords, raw cookies, session identifiers, CSRF values, configured secrets, private records or unnecessary personal data.
IF YOU DO NOT SEE THIS: record the real block as PENDING/BLOCKED/NOT_EXECUTED and retain a draft for teacher review; do not fabricate a conversation.
STOP CONDITION: unavailable access, disclosure risk, external testing or a request for the complete solution.
CONTINUE WITH: your own independent check and the same single S11 evidence PDF.

A reasoned UNKNOWN can demonstrate critical reflection. It does not establish implementation PASS. An alternative requires a distinct genuine prior teacher assessment contract; the form does not grant or authenticate it and this candidate keeps alternatives draft-only. No access-block penalty, grade or deadline is invented here.
