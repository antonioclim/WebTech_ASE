# S11 proposed marking rubric

**CANDIDATE. Not configured in Moodle and no grades awarded.** The source maxima 4/3/1.5/1.5 are preserved. Zero/partial/full levels make the planned evidence expectations explicit; partial credit does not establish normal completion or package FINAL admission.

If the teacher adopts Moodle Rubric with activity maximum 10, internal maxima 16/12/6/6 sum to 40 and grade = raw total/40×10. All minimum levels are 0. The actual institutional grade method, pass mark, deadline, attempts and penalties remain unset. No CSV direct import is claimed.

## Complete P02 and protective evidence

| Level | Points out of 10 | Internal points | Evidence descriptor |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Absent or unsupported | 0 | 0 | No assessable individual P02 evidence or claims unsupported by any locator. No actual execution credit for a model labelled as actual. |
| Partial and traceable | 2.0 | 8 | Traceable bounded individual implementation and some correctly classified protective cases, but compulsory cases/suites or provenance distinctions remain incomplete. Retain the omissions and limitations. |
| Complete and supported | 4 | 16 | Complete permitted one-file P02 work with actual final baseline 2/2, objective 5/5, regression 3/3 and infrastructure 10/10 PASS, separately recorded in the strict p02_checks ledger with actual sanitised locators; traceable allow/forbid/conceal/state, trusted-input, one-load and bounded-snapshot evidence. Models are labelled separately and do not replace the normal actual suite gate. |

Evidence fields: `principal_provenance`, `action_resource`, `trust_map`, `p02_diff`, `p02_completion`, `p02_checks`, `resource_loads`, `allow_observation`, `forbid_observation`, `conceal_observation`, `state_observation`, `principal_resource_independence`, `snapshot_observation`, `p02_unresolved`, `p02_evidence_index`.

| Level | Points out of 10 | Internal points | Evidence descriptor |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Absent or unsupported | 0 | 0 | No assessable CORS/CSRF evidence or an unbounded/all-five assertion without supporting records. |
| Partial and traceable | 1.5 | 6 | One class has coherent trace, benign controls and correctly limited evidence, while the other is incomplete; or both have useful partial records with explicit omissions. |

## Bounded Gemini critique

| Level | Points out of 10 | Internal points | Evidence descriptor |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Absent or unsupported | 0 | 0 | No genuine bounded exchange or no independent check. An actual access block is recorded honestly and referred to the real prior teacher assessment policy; no penalty or invented dialogue follows automatically from this descriptor. |
| Partial and traceable | 0.75 | 3 | Actual bounded excerpts and a falsifiable sanitised claim are recorded, but the independent check, verdict or limitation is incomplete. |
| Complete and supported | 1.5 | 6 | Genuine bounded actual exchange, independently checked claim, justified ACCEPTED/REJECTED/PARTIALLY_ACCEPTED/UNKNOWN verdict, correction and limitations. A reasoned UNKNOWN can earn critique credit; it does not establish implementation PASS. This candidate does not define alternative normal completion. |

Evidence fields: `ai_route`, `ai_claim`, `ai_prompt`, `ai_response`, `ai_check`, `ai_verdict`, `ai_correction_limit`.

| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |

Evidence fields: `student_alias`, `group`, `package_identity`, `actual_environment`, `execution_status`, `source_revision`, `p01_capstone`, `remaining_work`, `reflection`, `alternative_authority`, `authorship_decl`, `redaction_decl`, `pdf_review`, `submission_status`.

## Scope, incidents and authority

P02 technical correctness, reduced portfolio reasoning, model critique and traceability/reflection are different domains. Unsupported execution claims receive no actual-execution credit; a genuine limitation can support critical reflection. Avoid double counting the same merit merely because it has several locators.

A runtime incident, absent dependency, parser fault, timeout, crash or signal is not an intended starter assertion failure. Source/model fallback, incomplete work, access blocks and alternatives remain honest drafts here. Teacher prior policies may govern assessment of those records separately; no selected option, free-text approval or rubric silently grants equivalence. No full P01 or advanced all-five work is required.
