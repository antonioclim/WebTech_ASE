# S11 one-PDF Moodle route

**Candidate route for future course use.** The institutional Moodle version, actual S11 Assignment and its settings were not observed during production. These instructions do not create an activity or submit work. Use one individual private S11 Assignment and one reviewed PDF. There is no C11 Assignment or separate P03 upload.

## Prepare the local record

WHERE YOU ARE: the extracted `S11_STUDENT_v1.2.1` folder, not the ZIP preview.
WHAT TO FIND: `S11_EVIDENCE_FORM.html` (interactive text), `S11_EVIDENCE_FORM.docx` (genuine captures) and your own evidence.
EXACT ACTION: complete the 12 core fields in the meeting; retain unfinished work as an honest draft. Later finish actual P02 checks, the reduced CORS/CSRF portfolio and bounded Gemini critique.
WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE: your own text, field-entry counts and an explicit unverified state. Counts and selectors do not certify completion.
WHAT TO RECORD: package identity, measured environment, predicted/observed outcomes, evidence classes, locators and limitations.
DO NOT CONTINUE UNLESS: evidence is yours, sanitised and correctly classified; no model or access block has become an invented actual result.
IF YOU DO NOT SEE THIS: retain a draft, record the real incident and follow the actual teacher instruction.
STOP CONDITION: missing compulsory evidence, a runtime/provisioning gate or a privacy problem.
CONTINUE WITH: a current PDF candidate when the real course route permits it.

## Save and inspect one PDF

WHERE YOU ARE: the interactive form or your completed blank DOCX with your actual embedded captures.
WHAT TO FIND: `Print current draft` or, only for genuine normal completion, `Print standard final candidate`. The DOCX has no automatic validator; apply the checklist personally.
EXACT ACTION: use the current print button, choose the browser's actual PDF destination or Word's available PDF export and save one file. The native interface varies and was not qualified here. A native Ctrl+P event refreshes a labelled current draft unless a current standard final candidate was prepared.
WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE: an actual saved PDF containing all your evidence; never assume the dialog saved it.
WHAT TO RECORD: `pdf_review` reflects the real unsaved-candidate or reviewed-local-PDF state. Keep the JSON backup separately; do not upload it as the required PDF.
DO NOT CONTINUE UNLESS: every PDF page is readable, long text is intact, captures are embedded with captions and locators and secrets are removed.
IF YOU DO NOT SEE THIS: correct the document and export again. HTML is text-only, so use the DOCX route for genuine captures in the same PDF.
STOP CONDITION: a blank/old snapshot, clipped text, missing captures or any unreviewed page.
CONTINUE WITH: the actual named S11 Assignment supplied by your teacher.

Filename: `TW2026_S11_GROUP_ALIAS.pdf`. Use your real group and teacher-known private alias. The form removes diacritics, normalises separators and truncates each component to 45 characters. Check the actual saved name; avoid ambiguous aliases. Example naming syntax only: group 1001 and alias StudentA produce `TW2026_S11_1001_StudentA.pdf`. A filename does not authenticate identity. The teacher retains the private alias-to-student mapping.

## Upload to the actual S11 activity

WHERE YOU ARE: your authenticated institutional Moodle course, reached through the teacher's actual link.
WHAT TO FIND: the confirmed S11 activity, its published instructions, deadline, file limit and submission status. Do not infer them from this candidate.
EXACT ACTION: if the activity shows `Add submission`, open it, select the reviewed PDF and use the actual save control. If it instead shows an edit/retry path, follow the published activity instruction. A missing control or unexpected activity needs teacher clarification.
WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE: the actual file entry and submission status. Moodle may retain a draft or submit directly, depending on its configuration.
WHAT TO RECORD: the file/name, actual status and timestamp in your private evidence; remove unrelated personal data from any capture.
DO NOT CONTINUE UNLESS: it is the correct S11 activity and the actual PDF opens with the correct contents.
IF YOU DO NOT SEE THIS: do not duplicate-upload to C11/P03 or guess a different activity. Retain the local file and report the actual incident through the course's published route.
STOP CONDITION: wrong activity, filename/content mismatch, upload failure, expired permission or an unexpected declaration.
CONTINUE WITH: the activity's actual final submission step, if one is required.

## Confirm submission state

WHERE YOU ARE: the same actual activity after saving the PDF.
WHAT TO FIND: the displayed submission status. `Draft (not submitted)` and `Submitted for grading` are different states in documented Moodle variants.
EXACT ACTION: if the configured activity requires `Submit assignment`, use it only after personally reviewing the file and any real declaration. If the activity already reports submission, do not invent another step.
WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE: the actual submitted state and the correct file; saving a local PDF or passing form validation is not this receipt.
WHAT TO RECORD: set `submission_status` to ACTUAL_MOODLE_RECEIPT_RECORDED only after observing the real receipt. The local form cannot verify it.
DO NOT CONTINUE UNLESS: the institutional activity confirms the real state required by its instructions.
IF YOU DO NOT SEE THIS: preserve an honest DRAFT state and the actual incident; follow the teacher's real policy.
STOP CONDITION: unresolved submission state or an unintended irreversible submission/change.
CONTINUE WITH: no further upload unless the activity's genuine retry policy permits it.

## Unknown settings and evidence limits

Dates, cutoff, attempts, final-submit requirement, declarations, grade scale/pass threshold, feedback and institutional version remain unset in this package. Honest partial evidence and Gemini access blocks follow the teacher's actual prior policy; this candidate invents no penalty, approval or equivalence. A source/model fallback is a labelled draft, not normal complete work.
