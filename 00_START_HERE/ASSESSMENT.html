What you submit

Complete all two or three required projects in your seminar. Prepare the PDF required by that seminar and upload it only to the lecturer’s actual authorised Assignment. The public repository and its HTML forms do not receive Moodle submissions.

Build evidence while you work

  1. Read the project contract and identify the file you may edit.
  2. Record a useful starting observation, then implement your own change.
  3. Run the described task checks and capture their actual output. A collection integrity check is a separate check.
  4. Explain what the output demonstrates and what it leaves untested.
  5. Review the final PDF for the correct seminar, identity, project coverage and readable evidence.

Forms, drafts and private files

The seminar form helps you organise evidence; it is not proof that a test ran. A saved JSON draft is a private backup. Imported drafts retain their saved identity until deliberately reviewed and updated. Keep drafts, screenshots, logs, PDFs and own-case probes outside the entire extracted collection.

Honest conclusions

Report failures and unknown results as they occurred. A scoped passing check does not establish a working deployment, a successful Moodle submission or complete acceptance on another operating system. Each seminar requires one real AI exchange across its required projects, followed by your critique and an independent check. Do not invent a transcript. If access is unavailable, record BLOCKED with the actual reason and continue the independent work. Read evidence and PDF recovery.
