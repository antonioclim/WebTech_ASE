# LOCAL3: separate provisional classroom download

Version **3.0.0-rc.10-local.3** is a published prerelease with general qualification **NOT_FINAL**. Use this candidate when your lecturer selects LOCAL3 for class. The [frozen RC10 portal](../STUDENT_CLASSROOM_PUBLISHED_RC10/README.md) remains the default repository route.

## Prepare your classroom folder

1. Download [WEBTECH_ASE_LOCAL3_CLASSROOM.zip](https://github.com/antonioclim/WebTech_ASE/releases/download/classroom-en-gb-v3.0.0-rc.10-local.3/WEBTECH_ASE_LOCAL3_CLASSROOM.zip). Its [SHA-256 sidecar](https://github.com/antonioclim/WebTech_ASE/releases/download/classroom-en-gb-v3.0.0-rc.10-local.3/WEBTECH_ASE_LOCAL3_CLASSROOM.zip.sha256) is also available.
2. Extract the **complete ZIP** into a new folder and keep the folder structure intact. Do not merge it into an older collection.
3. Open `WEBTECH_LOCAL3/START_HERE.html`. Follow the setup page for your operating system, then the course and seminar links inside that collection.
4. Follow the classroom instructions for all two or three required individual projects. Keep your evidence, drafts, PDFs, logs and own synthetic probes outside the complete extracted collection, using the locations explained in the classroom guide.
5. Prepare the runtime before class. Review and submit your own evidence PDF through the private seminar assignment specified by your lecturer.
