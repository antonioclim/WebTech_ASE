What scoped verification can establish

Use the exact candidate manifests, identities, derivation record and test receipts to assess the named source and logic checks. Each result belongs to its actual files, runtime and test fixtures. Retained historical evidence is limited to the earlier bytes and cases identified by its receipt.

DOM simulation can test record state, handlers and programmatic focus. It does not establish rendered CSS, native keyboard traversal, an assistive-technology announcement or the browser's PDF save behaviour. An initial byte-verification PASS does not establish learner-task correctness.

Limits and private drafts

Native Windows and macOS, real browser layouts, Microsoft Word and PDF layout, live Moodle, a genuine student pilot and owner acceptance remain governed by their recorded gate states. No success is inferred merely from packaging or local derivation. The planned 30–45-minute blocks for every required project and the 90–120 or 120–165-minute total ranges are unpiloted. No measured learner completion time or empirical minimum is established. A shorter institutional slot does not remove a required task or certify completion.
